MONNY NATURALS SEO UPDATE
=========================
Production site: https://www.monnynatural.com

Added:
- Page-specific SEO titles and meta descriptions
- Canonical URLs
- Open Graph and Twitter sharing metadata
- Google-friendly robots directives
- Product JSON-LD structured data with NGN price and stock availability
- Breadcrumb structured data on product/category pages
- Organization and WebSite structured data
- Category SEO metadata
- noindex handling for admin, cart, checkout, filtered product searches and 404 pages
- client/public/robots.txt
- client/public/sitemap.xml
- Automatic sitemap generation before every Vite production build
- Server-side SEO head injection for direct visits to React routes

IMPORTANT AFTER DEPLOYMENT
--------------------------
1. Deploy the updated project to Vercel.
2. Confirm these URLs load:
   https://www.monnynatural.com/robots.txt
   https://www.monnynatural.com/sitemap.xml
3. In Google Search Console, verify monnynatural.com as a Domain property.
4. Submit: https://www.monnynatural.com/sitemap.xml
5. Use URL Inspection to request indexing for the homepage, /products, and important product pages.
6. Use Google's Rich Results Test on several product URLs after deployment.

No storefront design, cart logic, product content, or admin workflow was intentionally changed by this SEO update.
